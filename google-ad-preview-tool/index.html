AdCraft — Free Google Ad Preview Tool

AdCraft by LeadROI is a free Google Ad Preview Tool that lets you create, preview, and optimize your Google Ads before going live. Supports Responsive Search Ads (RSA), Performance Max, Display Ads, and Call-Only Ads — all with live SERP preview on desktop and mobile.

AI-Powered Ad Copy Generator

Built-in AI copy generator powered by Google Gemini creates high-converting headlines and descriptions for your Google Ads in seconds. Get 5 AI-generated headlines and 2 descriptions per format, all within Google's character limits — ready to use with one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Google Ad Preview Tool? +

A Google Ad Preview Tool lets you visualize exactly how your Google Ads will appear in search results before publishing. AdCraft shows real-time previews for RSA, Performance Max, Display, and Call-only formats on both desktop and mobile devices — so you can perfect your copy before spending a single rupee.

Is AdCraft completely free to use? +

Yes — AdCraft is 100% free. No signup, no credit card, no hidden limits. Open the tool, create your ads, and export your copy. The AI suggestions are powered by Google Gemini's free tier.

What Google Ads formats does AdCraft support? +

AdCraft supports all 4 major Google Ads formats: Responsive Search Ads (RSA) with up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions, Performance Max asset groups, Responsive Display Ads in multiple sizes, and Call-Only Ads. All formats include live preview, character limit checking, and CSV export.

How do I export my Google Ads copy? +

AdCraft offers two export options: Copy to Clipboard exports your ad copy in a formatted text layout with character counts, and Download CSV exports a Google Ads Editor-compatible file you can import directly into your Google Ads account — no reformatting needed.

What are Google Ads character limits? +

For Responsive Search Ads: Headlines are 30 characters max (up to 15 headlines), Descriptions are 90 characters max (up to 4 descriptions), and Display URL paths are 15 characters each. AdCraft enforces all these limits in real time with color-coded counters — green, amber, and red.
