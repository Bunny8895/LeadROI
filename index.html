FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with LeadROI.

What is the minimum ad budget I need? +

We recommend a minimum of ₹15,000–20,000/month ad spend for Google Ads to see meaningful results. For Meta Ads, ₹10,000/month can work well for local Hyderabad businesses. Note: this is separate from our management fee.

Is ₹10,000 the ad budget or your fee? +

₹10,000 is LeadROI's management fee only. Your ad budget is separate and goes directly to Google or Meta — you control it and we never touch your money.

How soon will I see results? +

Google Ads typically shows results within 2–4 weeks. Meta Ads can take 4–6 weeks to optimize properly as the algorithm learns. We set realistic expectations from day one and never overpromise.

What if I already have campaigns running? +

Perfect. We start with a free audit of your existing campaigns, show you exactly what's wrong and what's being wasted, and then restructure everything properly.

Do you work with small businesses? +

Yes — most of our clients are small and medium businesses in Hyderabad. Whether you're a clinic, real estate developer, education institute, or any local business, we've got experience in your space.

Do you guarantee results? +

No ethical agency can guarantee specific numbers — ad performance depends on your market, budget, landing page and many other factors. What we guarantee is full transparency, continuous optimization, honest reporting, and that we never run junk traffic to hit vanity metrics.
