Our Story
It started with a frustrated client call.

A business owner in Hyderabad called us after spending ₹40,000 on ads with another agency. He had 90 leads that month. He closed zero.

Not because his product was bad. Not because his pricing was wrong. Because every single lead was junk — wrong numbers, curious students, random clicks from people who had no intention of buying.

That call changed everything.

We realised the problem wasn't the ad budget. It wasn't even the ads. It was that most agencies optimise for lead volume — because that's what looks good on a report. High numbers. Impressive PDFs. Zero accountability for what actually happens after the lead comes in.

So we built LeadROI differently.
One obsession: quality over quantity.

We'd rather send you 20 leads that close than 200 leads that waste your time. That's not a tagline. That's literally how we measure our own success — by your revenue, not our reports.

Today LeadROI manages Google Ads, Meta Ads and landing pages for businesses across Hyderabad — from real estate developers to clinic owners to EdTech startups.

The goal has never changed:
Leads that bring ROI. Not leads that bring chaos.
